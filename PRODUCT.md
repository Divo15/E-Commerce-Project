# Shali Collections

## Register

brand

## Users

Customers shopping for premium Indian ethnic fashion and jewellery for everyday
wear, weddings, festivals, and gifting.

## Product Purpose

Help customers discover collections and purchase the right products and sizes
through a clear, trustworthy storefront.

## Brand Personality

Elegant, celebratory, premium. Preserve the existing Shali identity and voice.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic electronics-marketplace styling, harsh neon accents, and layouts
that obscure the richness of ethnic fashion.

## Design Principles

- Keep departments, categories, products, and availability driven by real data.
- Make browsing and checkout clear on mobile and desktop.
- Give customers accurate feedback about sizes, stock, and order status.
- Use the established storefront layout and visual system for new pages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA, keyboard navigation, readable forms, descriptive labels,
accessible validation errors, and reduced-motion support.

Detailed catalog requirements remain in `ecommerce/templates/PRODUCT.md`.
Existing storefront implementation notes remain in `HOMEPAGE.md`.

## Verified Implementation — 2026-10-15

The current application supports catalog search, product galleries, stock-aware
size selection, session carts and navigation counts, account forms, and shipping
drafts. Product and account pages use the established storefront layout.
Payments, delivery rates/serviceability, order notifications, catalog/cart visual
integration, and production deployment remain unfinished.
